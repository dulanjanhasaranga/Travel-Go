1. Executive Summary
Travel GO is a company which works in the field of travel and tourism and offers booking of tour packages, help with visa applications, arrangements for flight tickets, and optional hotel accommodation to individuals, families and small tour groups. At the moment the agency makes use of manual, paper-based methods and separate spreadsheets for managing tour bookings, visa applications, customer records and travel packages.
The Business Architecture Assessment looks at the current (As-Is) business processes at TravelGo in four main operational areas which correspond directly to the system epics: (1) User, Staff and System Administration, (2) Catalogue Management (covering Package, Destination and Hotel), (3) Booking and Customer Journey, and (4) Visa and Payment Management.
The main findings indicate that the present procedures involve manual transfers between staff members, repeated data entry, slow cycles when verifying visa documents, inconsistent timelines for sending out booking confirmations, and no real-time visibility regarding booking, payment and visa status for either the staff or the customers. These inefficiencies result in delays in processing, occasional mistakes and lower levels of customer satisfaction, as well as imposing a large administrative workload on a small staff team.
The idea is to substitute the existing manual procedures with an integrated, role-based web platform (Travel GO) which will automate the creation of bookings, the tracking of visas, the simulation of payment processing and the publication of packages and hotels, while at the same time keeping the approval and verification stages that were identified in this assessment. The results serve as the basis for the later Information, Application and Technology Architecture reviews.
2. Organization Profile
Organization Name
Travel GO – Smart Travel Agency
Industry Sector
Travel and tourism services (travel agency and tour operations).
Size
Small-to-medium sized travel agency operating from a head office, with a small team of booking officers, visa processing officers, finance staff, package/destination administrators and management. Serves individual travelers, families and small tour groups.
Products / Services
Domestic and international tour package bookings
Visa type guidance, document handling and visa application processing
Flight ticket information as part of tour packages
Optional hotel accommodation offered as an add-on during booking
Customer inquiry handling, reviews and ratings
Organizational Structure
Travel GO is organized around four functional areas that map to the system epics: Administration & User Management, Catalogue (Package/Destination/Hotel) Management, Booking & Customer Management, and Visa & Payment Management, with overall direction provided by senior management/ownership.
Current Business Environment
The agency operates in a competitive market shaped by online travel aggregators (OTAs), rising customer expectations for online self-service and real-time status tracking, and growing demand for combined package + visa + optional-hotel bookings handled through a single channel.
Existing Information Systems
Operations are currently supported mainly by manual paper-based processes, basic spreadsheets, email and phone communication with customers and partners. There is no centralized customer, booking or visa database and no role-based access control across departments.
3. Business Problem Statement
Many travel agencies, such as Travel GO, make use of manual methods or separate, individual systems when they are arranging tour bookings, processing visa applications and putting together travel packages. As a result, there are operational inefficiencies, inaccurate or out-of-date information, and a lack of consistency in customer service. The people who handle bookings and those who deal with visas have to spend a lot of time re-entering data onto spreadsheets and paper forms. Customers do not have the ability to look themselves up and check the status of their bookings or visa applications, and managers have very little insight into the performance of operations throughout the various departments.
In order to enable customers to browse travel packages, book tours, apply for visas and keep track of their requests all via the internet, agency staff are to manage tour bookings, visa applications, customer records and travel packages using role-based access; this is anticipated to lead to greater operational efficiency, improved service quality and higher customer satisfaction.
This assessment covers the customer's processes, booking, visa, payment (simulated) and administrative management, including information regarding flight tickets and optional hotel accommodation; hotel accommodation is not included in the standard package price and is available as an optional extra. The integration of a live payment gateway is not within the scope of this academic project; payment is instead represented as a simulated process.

14. Process Assessment
The current User, Staff & System Administration process operates mainly through manual procedures, spreadsheets, paper records, email and informal approval methods. When a customer or staff member requires an account, a request is submitted to an administrator who reviews the information, checks for missing details and duplicates, and determines the required role. Sensitive access requests may require management approval.
Main delays occur during transfer of requests, correction of incomplete information, and management approval. Because communication is not centralized, requests may remain unanswered. Customers and newly recruited staff are most affected. Priority improvement areas are automated duplicate checking, structured request tracking, and clear digital approval records.

The current Package, Destination and Hotel Management process relies heavily on staff to create, update and maintain travel information. Destination descriptions and images, package details, pricing, availability, included services, flight information and hotel information must be maintained accurately. Use of manual procedures and disconnected spreadsheets increases the risk of duplicated data, outdated information and inconsistent pricing or availability.

14. Process Assessment
The current visa application process depends heavily on the Visa Officer manually checking document completeness and authenticity, with no automated checklist to catch missing items before the officer’s review begins. Every incomplete submission consumes a full review cycle before the applicant is told what is missing.
Major delays occur at document verification and at reconciling payment confirmation with application status, since these are tracked in separate spreadsheets. Applicants and Visa Officers are the most affected. Manually cross-referencing payment records against visa applications creates no direct business value and is a strong candidate for automation. Priority should be given to automated document-completeness checking and linking payment confirmation directly to application status. Before designing the To-Be process, evidence should be collected on typical document-rejection rates, average processing times by visa type, and the frequency of high-value fee cases.
