Ask our team ↗
Do tour package prices include hotel accommodation?

Hotel accommodation is selected separately during booking. If you choose a hotel, its cost is added to the package price and shown in your booking summary before you submit.

What is included in the base tour package price?

Inclusions vary by package. Check the package details for flights, transport, activities and excluded services. Visa and documentation charges are quoted separately after document review.

Can visitors browse destination and tour information without logging in?

Yes. You can browse destinations, packages, the visa guide and contact details without signing in. An account is required to manage a booking or visa application.

How is visa document verification handled?

A visa officer reviews the documents uploaded to your application, requests further documents when needed and issues the visa and documentation charges after verification. You can follow the application status in your account. This website does not submit applications directly to embassies.
