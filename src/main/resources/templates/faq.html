Frequently Asked Questions

Key answers regarding package pricing, hotel policies, visa processing, and account access

Do tour package prices include hotel accommodation?

No. In accordance with our core business rules, package prices do not include hotel accommodation. Tour package prices cover round-trip flights, visa guidance, and guided activities. Hotel accommodation is an optional add-on that travelers can customize during booking based on their preferred star rating.

What is included in the base tour package price?

Every standard tour package includes return economy flights, standard visa assistance support, skip-the-line entrance tickets for key attractions, and private ground transportation between scheduled itinerary highlights.

Can visitors browse destination and tour information without logging in?

Yes! All visitors can freely browse destinations, examine package prices, read visa criteria, check agency contact information, and review business hours without creating an account or logging in.

How is visa document verification handled?

Visa processing is managed independently through our dedicated Visa Management module. Certified Visa Officers inspect uploaded documents against destination country regulations before embassy submission.
