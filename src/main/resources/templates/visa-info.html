Know what comes next

Your visa journey

Every TravelGO booking requires an approved visa before final payment. Follow your application from your customer dashboard.

01 · Booking & documents

Start with your travel request

Choose your package, travel date and travelers. Open Visa applications in your dashboard and apply against your booking.

Upload PDF, JPEG or PNG files up to 10 MB each. Your booking owner and visa officers can download them. Uploads are accepted only while document submission is open.

02 · Verification & quote

Review before payment

A visa officer assesses your documents before issuing the visa and documentation charges. Check the quoted breakdown and the deadline shown on your application.

The upfront quote is payable for 24 hours after issue. If it expires unpaid, ask an officer to reissue it.

03 · Processing & decision

Follow your application

Successful upfront payment starts processing. The officer records approval or rejection; rejection includes a reason.

A rejection cancels the booking and refunds the documentation charge from the successful upfront payment. The visa charge is not refunded by this workflow.

04 · Final payment & confirmation

Confirm your trip

After visa approval, pay the package and any selected hotel costs by the booking’s deadline, three days before departure. Successful full payment completes confirmation.

Changes and cancellations are available only while the booking is pending and visa review or payment has not started.

Prepare with your travel consultant

Document requirements and processing times depend on your destination, nationality and travel purpose. Confirm the current official requirements with the relevant consulate and your consultant. TravelGO does not guarantee an embassy decision or an appointment.

This project records staff-managed visa decisions and simulated payments. It does not submit applications directly to embassies.
